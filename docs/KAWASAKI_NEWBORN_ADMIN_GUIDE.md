# Kawasaki Newborn Admin Guide

**Last verified:** 2026-09-17  
**Scope:** Kawasaki City / Kawasaki Ward, for the first administrative steps after a baby is born.

> This is a practical checklist for Peggy. Rules can change, so re-check the linked Kawasaki City pages if using this later.

## Best same-day order at the ward office

1. **Birth Registration — 出生届**
2. **Apply for the baby's My Number Card at the same time — マイナンバーカード**
3. **Child Allowance — 児童手当**
4. **Baby's health insurance enrollment** if using National Health Insurance — 国民健康保険
5. **Child Medical Expense Subsidy — 小児医療費助成 / 小児医療証**
6. **Newborn contact/home-visit form — 出生連絡票 / 赤ちゃん訪問**

Useful phrase at the first counter:

> 出生届と一緒に、子どものマイナンバーカードも申請したいです。児童手当と小児医療費助成の手続きもしたいです。
>
> *Shussei todoke to issho ni, kodomo no Mainanbā kādo mo shinsei shitai desu. Jidō teate to shōni iryōhi josei no tetsuzuki mo shitai desu.*

## 1. Birth Registration — 出生届

- **Deadline:** within 14 days, counting the day of birth as day 1.
- The official notifier is normally the **father or mother** when the parents are married. If the parents are not married, the mother is the notifier.
- **The person physically bringing the form to the counter does not have to be both parents.** The father can go alone; the mother and baby do not need to come just to submit the birth registration.
- Submit at the ward office for the baby's address, registered domicile, place of birth, or temporary place of stay.
- Bring:
  - 出生届 with the hospital/doctor-completed 出生証明書
  - 母子健康手帳 (Boshi Techo)
  - If enrolled in Kawasaki National Health Insurance, bring the relevant qualification document if available.
- The **出生届出済証明** section in the Boshi Techo is completed/certified by the ward office, not by the parents.
- If the birth registration is submitted outside normal counter hours, other procedures such as child allowance normally have to be completed later on an open business day.

Official Kawasaki City source:  
https://www.city.kawasaki.jp/250/page/0000037114.html

## 2. Baby's My Number Card — apply together with 出生届

For a newborn, the fastest path is to submit the My Number Card application **at the same time as the birth registration**.

- Ask for/use **個人番号カード交付申請書兼電子証明書発行申請書（出生届時用）** if the application section is not already integrated into the form you have.
- When applying together with the birth registration:
  - The baby **does not need to come to the ward office**.
  - The baby's separate identity documents are **not required** for that simultaneous application.
  - For a child under 1 year old, the card is issued **without a face photo**, so no baby photo is needed.
- Newborns are eligible for **特急発行 (express issuance)**. Kawasaki says the card is generally mailed directly to the registered address in **about one week**, but this is not guaranteed and can take longer.
- The baby's My Number itself is assigned after the birth registration/resident record process. The **個人番号通知書** is mailed to the registered address. If the My Number Card was applied for together with the birth registration, Kawasaki says the notice is enclosed with the card.
- If you submit outside normal counter hours or at a municipality other than the registered-address municipality, delivery can take longer than one week.

Official Kawasaki City source:  
https://www.city.kawasaki.jp/250/page/0000171234.html

## 3. Child Allowance — 児童手当

- **Application is required; it is not automatic.**
- For a newborn, apply as soon as possible.
- If the application is made within **15 days from the day after the birth date**, the benefit can start from the month following the birth month under the timing rule.
- A late application can mean losing payments for earlier months because Kawasaki generally does not pay retroactively for late applications.

Official Kawasaki City source:  
https://www.city.kawasaki.jp/450/page/0000030462.html

## 4. Baby's health insurance

### If the baby will join Kawasaki National Health Insurance — 国民健康保険

- Report the enrollment event **within 14 days**.
- Do this through the ward office's insurance/pension section after the birth registration is processed.
- Since the old physical health-insurance-card system has changed, the baby may receive/use **資格情報のお知らせ** or **資格確認書** depending on My Number health-insurance registration status.

Official Kawasaki City source:  
https://www.city.kawasaki.jp/350/page/0000139064.html

### If the baby will be a dependent on employer health insurance — 社会保険

- This is **not enrolled through Kawasaki City**.
- Apply through the insured parent's employer / health insurance association for dependent enrollment (被扶養者).
- Ask HR what documents they need after the birth registration is accepted.

## 5. Child Medical Expense Subsidy — 小児医療費助成

**Important 2026 update:** From **September 2026**, Kawasaki expanded the program.

- Eligible children: **age 0 through high-school age** (through the first March 31 after turning 18), subject to program rules.
- The child must be enrolled in health insurance.
- Kawasaki states there is **no income limit** for this program.
- Covered insured outpatient, prescription, and inpatient self-pay amounts are subsidized, excluding items that are outside health insurance and certain other excluded costs.
- Normally the parent with the higher income is the applicant/guardian for this program.

Official Kawasaki City source, updated 2026-09-01:  
https://www.city.kawasaki.jp/450/page/0000128564.html

## 6. Newborn contact / home visit — 出生連絡票・赤ちゃん訪問

- Kawasaki asks families to submit the **出生連絡票** from the Boshi Techo, or apply online, **within 14 days after birth**.
- A public-health nurse, midwife, nurse, or other assigned staff can contact/visit the family to check the baby's growth and the mother's condition and provide parenting support.

Official Kawasaki City source:  
https://www.city.kawasaki.jp/450/page/0000030575.html

## What to bring for a one-trip attempt

- Completed 出生届 + hospital-completed 出生証明書
- 母子健康手帳
- Parent's photo ID / residence card
- Parent's My Number Card if available
- Health-insurance qualification information/documents currently available
- Bank account details for the parent who will receive 児童手当, if requested by the counter
- Phone with access to any employer/insurance information you may need

Document requirements can vary by household and insurance type, so the counter may request additional documents.

## Counter strategy

Tell the first staff member you want to finish **all newborn procedures in one visit**. Do not leave after the 出生届 counter until you have confirmed where to go next for:

- マイナンバーカード
- 児童手当
- 国民健康保険 (only if applicable)
- 小児医療費助成
- 出生連絡票 / 赤ちゃん訪問

This guide intentionally separates **My Number assignment**, **My Number Card issuance**, **health-insurance enrollment**, and **medical-subsidy enrollment** because they are different procedures even though they can be handled around the same time.
